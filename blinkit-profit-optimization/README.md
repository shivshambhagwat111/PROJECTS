# Blinkit profit optimization: business case study

Independent analysis of where 10-minute quick-commerce delivery leaks profit (Oct - Nov 2024). The deck is `BlinkIt-Profit-Optimization-Deck.pdf`.

- **Method:** contribution-margin-per-order driver tree framed with Eternal's public disclosures (the per-order cost and revenue figures in the deck are an illustrative index, not company data), plus 16 user interviews (7 frequent, 5 moderate, 4 occasional/lapsed users) recruited through personal connections in Delhi NCR and Reddit.
- **Levers proposed:** checkout nudges, membership, a Rs 3 bag fee, dynamic delivery pricing and retail media, sequenced by risk and effort.
- **Findings:** 14 of 16 accepted a Rs 3 bag fee with an opt-out while 11 of 16 rejected it as a silent fee; 12 of 16 preferred threshold-based nudges; heavy users said they would pay Rs 150-250 a month to drop delivery fees.
- **Limitations:** built from public data only, with no access to internal order-level costs, so the diagnosis is directional. The 16 interviews are a small convenience sample measuring stated preference, so the bag-fee and membership findings are signals to test, not validated results.
