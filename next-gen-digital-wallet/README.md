# Next-Gen Digital Wallet: product case study

Self-directed product case study on multi-currency wallets (Feb - Mar 2025). The full write-up is in `Next-Gen-Digital-Wallet-Case-Study.pdf`.

- **Problem:** multi-currency wallets move money well but do little to help users understand it, so retention is weak.
- **Research:** 5-app teardown, 118-user survey and 7 follow-up interviews. 68% rated cross-currency bill splitting 4-5/5 in frustration.
- **Prioritization:** RICE scoring (AI Budgeting 45.0, AR Bill Splitting 21.6) combined with the survey pain evidence chose the two MVP features. Rewards, investments and a converter widget were cut; the converter scored 40.5 on RICE but was dropped as table stakes, so RICE informed the choice rather than decided it.
- **Design:** Figma prototypes with a non-AR fallback flow.
- **Impact model:** 30-day retention from 25% to 29.8% (+19% relative), built from 64% stated intent discounted 50% and an assumed 40% retention among adopters. This is a projection to validate with an A/B test, not a measured result.
